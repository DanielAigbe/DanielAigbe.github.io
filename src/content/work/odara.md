## The problem

Odara's own pitch starts from a gap: by its figures, over 70% of Nigerian healthcare is paid out of pocket, and hospitals wait 60–120 days for HMO reimbursement. Odara offers four credit products for four different audiences: hospitals waiting on HMO payments, pharma distributors, equipment buyers and patients paying for care.

The site had to explain all four without becoming hard to navigate, and give each audience a clear route to checking whether they qualify. The client asked for a full website.

## My role

I designed the landing page, the About and How It Works pages, a page for each of the four products (Pay, Equip, Receivables and Capital), and a short eligibility flow. The pages are built from a shared component set.

## Key decisions

- **A page for each product.** Each product serves a different need and a different audience. Giving each its own page lets it explain itself clearly, instead of crowding all four onto one page.
- **One template for all four.** Every product page follows the same structure and uses the same components, so the site stays consistent and a visitor who has read one product page already knows how to read the others.
- **Every product page leads to the eligibility check.** Each one has a "Check eligibility" button that opens a short eligibility flow, so a visitor can go from "what is this?" to "do I qualify?" without hunting for it.
