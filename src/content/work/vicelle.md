## The brief

Vicelle Clothing is a fashion label that mixes minimal luxury with expressive design. The brand stands for confidence, originality and a clean look rooted in a modern African identity.

The label was brand new, with no logo or identity yet. The client needed a foundation for the brand: a logo and the visual system around it, refined and simple, that would make customers feel valued and seen.

## My role

I designed the identity and wrote it up as a 24-page brand guide: the logo and its variations, the rules for using it, the colour palette, the typography, a brand pattern, and mockups showing it in use. The guide gives the label one consistent look across its website and social pages, clothing labels, packaging and adverts.

## Key decisions

- **A modern African identity, without the usual African visual cues.** The brief asked for a clean, minimal look rooted in a modern African identity. I kept it restrained: a clean wordmark, a two-tone V and a simple slash pattern, instead of traditional motifs.
- **One mark that works at every size.** The V sits above the wordmark in the full logo, and also works alone as a small mark for neck labels, hang tags and profile pictures, where the full name would be too small to read.
- **A pattern taken from the logo.** The slashes use the angle of the V, so the brand can show up on tags, bags and banners without putting the full logo on every surface.

## The logo

![Vicelle Clothing main logo: a navy wordmark with a turquoise and navy V above it](/images/vicelle/logo.webp)

A modern wordmark with a V that is half turquoise, half navy.

![The logo shown on white, navy, black and patterned backgrounds](/images/vicelle/backgrounds.webp)

The logo on light, dark and patterned backgrounds. The turquoise half of the V stays the same on every one, so the mark stays recognisable.

![Logo construction lines showing the clear space around the logo](/images/vicelle/spacing.webp)

Clear space around the logo equals the height of the V. The smallest allowed width is 120px.

## Colour and type

![Colour palette: turquoise, white, black and deep navy](/images/vicelle/colours.webp)

Turquoise (#19C6C1) leads as the fresh, attention-getting colour. Deep navy (#1E2A38) sits beside it to add depth and keep the brand calm. Black and white handle text and backgrounds, so the palette stays simple.

![Proxima Nova type specimen in turquoise](/images/vicelle/typeface.webp)

Proxima Nova for headlines and key messages, Inter for body text, product descriptions and captions.
