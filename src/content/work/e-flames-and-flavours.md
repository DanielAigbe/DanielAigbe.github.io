## The brief

e-Flames & Flavours is a contemporary Nigerian restaurant brand built around authentic food cooked over open flames, generous portions and warm hospitality. Its audience is urban professionals, students and young families who want Nigerian food in a modern setting.

The brand needed an identity that felt as warm and confident as the food. The client asked me to handle the branding, and shared other brands whose style they liked as a starting point.

## My role

I built the full identity and wrote it up as a brand guide: the logo system and its usage rules, the colour palette, the typography, image guidelines, real-world applications and the menu designs. The colours are also set up as design tokens in Figma.

I worked on it alone, from the first sketches to the finished guide, over a little more than a week.

## Key decisions

- **Three values drive every choice: Real, Warm, Bold.** Real means honest Nigerian cooking with nothing dressed up. Warm means every touchpoint should feel like a welcome. Bold means the brand shows up with confidence, colour and heat. Together they set the direction for the whole identity.
- **A mascot built from the letter "e".** The "e" becomes a winking chef with a flame for a hat, so the name, the food and the personality sit in one mark. I started with several logo directions and got the client's feedback on what worked and what didn't, then combined the strongest parts. They liked the first mascot and asked for one tweak, and that tweak became the mascot used today.
- **Colours named after the kitchen.** Chef Cream is the welcoming base, Flame Orange carries the heat and energy, Maroon Red adds richness, and Ember Brown is the deep supporting tone. Naming them this way makes the palette easy to remember and use for a client working with the guide without a designer.

## The logo

![The e-Flames & Flavours logo on a cream background](/images/eflames/logo.webp)

The logo comes in three versions: the full lockup, the wordmark alone, and the chef icon alone. The full lockup is always the first choice. The icon and wordmark are for places where the lockup can't fit.

![The three logo versions: icon, wordmark and full lockup, on maroon](/images/eflames/logo-system.webp)

The guide sets clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "e". The logo is drawn to stay legible on both light and dark backgrounds.

![The logo on a maroon sign and on a white sign](/images/eflames/signage.webp)

## Typography

The "e-" and "& Flavours" in the wordmark are custom lettering drawn for the brand. "Flames" is set in a script, and Baloo 2 handles headings and body text across the guide and menus.

![A specimen of the custom lettering beside the logo on maroon](/images/eflames/typeface.webp)

## In use

The guide shows the identity across the places people will meet it: restaurant signage, a takeaway food box, a takeaway bag, a staff apron and cap, and the menu. It also sets three rules for photography: the food is the hero, the colours stay warm, and people are shown sharing the meal.

## Outcome

The client loved the full identity, from the mascot to the rest of the brand guide.

The identity is complete, but the restaurant has not launched yet. Packaging and menus are the first places it will be used.
